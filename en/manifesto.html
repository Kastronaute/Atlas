The Manifesto

the human voice

I'm the most ordinary there is.
I wake up, and have my coffee, same as you.
I'm not what you would call "someone who succeeded", I'm not "arrived somewhere" where you would want to be, and I have no skill you would not possess.

I grew up, same as you.
In a world having us believe that game should be abandoned on the road.
That we must grow, and that to play can't produce, nothing.
Yet, I'm playing, still...

I'm in the same perpetual movement since ever, mine.
Without once knowing where it led me, nor where it will.
And I know just as much as you today, I'm not eager to know either.
I wanna lead no one nowhere, there is no story of a leader.

Inherently, our will is to play!
At birth, we play a lot and with a lot of people, then we grow up...
I wanted to keep playing when work had them grow, for those who are still alive.
And here, I'd like to thank those that the road has taken from me.
In time, there were few with the will to play, inherent.

We all burn out!
We lose hope that playing everywhere, is possible anytime.
And we endure the taming of productivity, all.
To grow up we must produce, they know.
There is no "they".
It's a pool.
A pool to be born into, all.
A pool to tame us, all.
And this pool listens to neither you, nor your will.
We shouldn't mind the pool, never.

There is truth in the pool only amidst our echo and those that go with us, alive or dead.

And to those who came before me, leaving the truth of the words that allowed me to escape — whom I can't thank, for the list is too long and would burn out all of us —
I want to testify my most sincere, whole, and infinite gratitude.

I thanked everyone; this reading will soon end, don't worry.
Me, I have no wish to burn you out.
I just wanna play, still!

This is not the story of a pool that tames your inherent wills.
This is the story of a player, who carefully listens.
And you?
